# Ralph Risk Score

The Ralph risk score is a 0–5 severity scale used to classify operational and safety issues. Scores 0–2 represent low concern where normal procedures apply. Score 3 is subdivided into 3a, 3b, 3c, and 3d to distinguish gradations of moderate risk that require escalating oversight but do not halt work. Scores 4 and above indicate critical conditions; in particular, a risk score of 5, denoted STOP, means all affected operations must cease immediately until the hazard is mitigated and cleared.
