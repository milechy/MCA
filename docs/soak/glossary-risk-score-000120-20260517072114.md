The Ralph Risk Score is a scale from 0 to 5 used to classify the severity and required handling of changes or anomalies. A score of 0 indicates no risk, while scores of 1 and 2 represent low and moderate concerns respectively. Risk level 3 is subdivided into four tiers—3a, 3b, 3c, and 3d—to provide finer gradation within the significant-risk band, reflecting increasing urgency or complexity. A score of 4 signals a critical risk requiring immediate attention. Risk 5 is an absolute stop condition that halts all related activity until the issue is resolved.
