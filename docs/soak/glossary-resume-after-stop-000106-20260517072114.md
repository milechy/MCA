The resume-after-security-stop workflow is a manual, admin-only procedure that allows a privileged operator to explicitly re-enable operations after an automated security halt. This mechanism is intentionally restricted to single-use recovery actions to prevent repeated or automated circumvention of safety controls. It must never be invoked as part of a full-auto sequence or scheduled agentic loop, ensuring a human remains firmly in the loop. Activation requires administrative credentials and is audited to maintain accountability for overriding the stop state. Once consumed, the single-use token or flag must be regenerated through a separate, deliberate authorization step. This design guarantees that security stops remain meaningful and cannot be silently bypassed by non-privileged or autonomous processes.
