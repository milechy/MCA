# Ralph Risk Score (0..5)

The Ralph risk score is a 0-to-5 scale used to classify the severity and uncertainty of a proposed change. Risk 0 denotes trivial, no-impact edits, while Risks 1 and 2 cover low-to-moderate complexity with a contained blast radius. Risk 3 is further split into 3a, 3b, 3c, and 3d, representing increasing levels of complexity, unknowns, or potential downstream impact. Risk 4 marks high-stakes changes that demand extensive validation and careful review before proceeding. Risk 5 means STOP: the change is deemed too dangerous to execute and must be blocked or escalated immediately.
